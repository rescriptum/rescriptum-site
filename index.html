We build tools that let people rewrite what they have captured.

Rescriptum is a software company making creative AI tools for people who work with pictures, video and voice. Our products ship under the Imagenta brand.

What we build

Everyday creative tools, built on frontier models.

Pictures

Generate new images from a description, or take a photo you already have and change what is in it: swap backgrounds, remove objects, restyle, upscale.

Video

Turn a still image or a prompt into a short clip, extend and edit footage, and prepare it for the places people actually post it.

Voice

Natural text-to-speech in many languages and voices, for narration, product videos, learning material and accessibility.

Product

One place for the whole creative routine.

MADE WITH IMAGENTA
Imagenta
imagenta.ai

Imagenta.ai is a web platform where people create and edit photos and video, generate voice from text, and use a growing set of small creative tools, all in one account. It is built, operated and supported by Rescriptum.

  • Image generationRealistic images from a text description.
  • Photo editingChange, extend or clean up an existing picture.
  • Video generationShort clips from an image or a prompt.
  • Text to speechLifelike voice in many languages.
  • Background removalClean cut-outs in one click.
  • Creative toolsSmall utilities for everyday tasks, added regularly.

How we work

A small team, running the whole company with AI.

We are an AI-native company in the plain sense: not only are our products built on frontier models, the company itself is run with them. Engineering, design, analytics and marketing all work through Claude every day.

Engineering

The service is built and maintained in Claude Code, with agentic workflows for coding, review and tooling.

Design

Interfaces, assets and marketing visuals are designed and iterated together with Claude.

Analytics

Product and revenue data from Google Analytics and Stripe is analysed through Claude and custom MCP integrations.

Marketing

Ad campaigns are launched, monitored and optimised through Claude connected to Google Ads.
